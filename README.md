Set in an early-2000s American town, the game follows Sam through a coming-of-age story about friendship, romance, rebellion, and his plan to rock the school.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Agefield High: Rock the School**. This trainer enhances the game's school-life and open-world systems with optional controls for player survival, money, time management, attendance, grades, and police attention. It is designed for the single-player PC version and supports experimentation across the main missions, side activities, lessons, shops, and open-world exploration.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to replay the 8 to 10 hour campaign, explore Agefield freely, experiment with the school schedule, complete side missions, afford customization items, or reduce the difficulty of combat and police encounters.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Health** — Prevents Sam from losing health during fights, risky activities, and encounters around Agefield.
- **Unlimited Stamina** — Allows extended movement and activities without normal stamina limitations.
- **Add Pocket Money** — Adds money for purchases such as clothes, bikes, hairstyles, tattoos, accessories, and other shop items.
- **Freeze School Time** — Pauses the time and school schedule so you can explore, complete objectives, or prepare for lessons without advancing the day.
- **Ignore Attendance** — Reduces the impact of skipping classes while you pursue missions and activities during school hours.
- **Improve Grades** — Adjusts grade-related progress connected to the game's English, Math, Geography, German, and Music lessons.
- **Disable Police Attention** — Suppresses police response and reduces the consequences of unlawful actions during free-roam activities.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Easy Combat** — Makes the game's intentionally straightforward combat easier by combining unlimited health, stamina support, and optional damage assistance.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch the game and load a save before activating movement-related options. Unlimited stamina is useful when travelling across the school campus, neighborhoods, town center, and countryside. Freeze school time before leaving campus if you want to explore during weekday class hours without advancing the schedule.

### Tools & Inventory

Use Add Pocket Money before visiting shops. The game uses money for bikes, clothing, hairstyles, tattoos, accessories, and other purchases. Buy items normally through the in-game shops after activating the trainer option, and avoid changing values while a purchase transaction is in progress.

### World & Environment

Agefield is a small open world with a school campus, neighborhoods, town center, and countryside. Freeze School Time gives you more freedom to explore between objectives, while Ignore Attendance is intended for runs focused on side missions and activities rather than regular classes. Disable Police Attention can be used when testing unlawful actions or exploring without interruptions.

### Combat & Weapons

Activate Unlimited Health before confrontations and Unlimited Stamina for extended encounters. Easy Combat is intended for the game's simple action-based fights and should be enabled before entering a mission area. Turn combat options off when you want the normal challenge and consequences.
